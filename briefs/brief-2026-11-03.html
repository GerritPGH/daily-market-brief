Section 1What matters today

Oil gave back its war premium. Brent settled 7.6% lower at $104.77 on Monday after Iran’s supreme leader authorised talks, its biggest fall in six months, and the end-2027 contract now sits at a record $87 (FT; Bloomberg; Authers, opinion). Overnight the US struck targets on Iran’s Qeshm Island, on the eve of today’s midterms (FT). The AI sell-off kept going: the Nasdaq 100 fell 1.39% while the Dow rose 0.41% (AP; CappNotes), and Anthropic disclosed third-quarter revenue of almost $17bn against a $23.3bn net loss and more than $895bn of compute commitments, weeks before a Nasdaq listing that could raise at least $100bn (Bloomberg; WSJ). Gilts rallied 9bp to 5.20% at ten years (TE). In Johannesburg miners lifted the All Share 1.77%; then Washington moved to drop South Africa from Agoa from next year (Sharenet; Daily Maverick). This morning Australia raised rates to 4.85% and Seoul is down 3.2% (RBA; TE). Our chains: the East London car chain dies on naamsa’s export print, and two new chains follow the oil unwind into Thursday’s Bank of England vote and Anthropic’s IPO into a supply test for AI shares.
