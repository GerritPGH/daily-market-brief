Section 1What matters today

Two stories pulled in opposite directions. On Wednesday the AI trade came back: Anthropic’s IPO drew ‘multiple times’ the shares on offer (Bloomberg), CoreWeave jumped 24.71% on a raised forecast (Motley Fool) and the Nasdaq 100 rose 2.28% to 28,374.25, with the S&P 500 up 1.31% to 7,619.38 (CappNotes). Nvidia closed 2.87% higher at $173.73, then fell 8.61% after hours (Stocktitan; FT) on reports that its board is weighing Jensen Huang’s future after a second relationship came to light; the board is working with an outside firm on candidates (Bloomberg). Nasdaq 100 futures were 1.1% lower at 06:02 GMT (Bloomberg). The cash Treasury market was shut for Veterans Day, so US yields have no new close. South Africa did not share the rally: the All Share fell 1.08% and retailers 3.62% as Woolworths had its worst day since 2020 (Sharenet; Business Day), and the ten-year rose 10.5bp to 8.61% (TE). Saudi Arabia told OPEC its October output rose 26% to 10.56m b/d (Bloomberg); Brent still settled 1.39% higher at $92.76 (CappNotes). Today: UK third-quarter GDP at 07:00 and the US distillate print that settles our diesel chain. Two new chains: Nvidia’s scandal as a key-man discount, not an AI break, and Australia’s David Jones collapse as a Woolworths story.
