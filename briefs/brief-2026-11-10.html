Section 1What matters today

Relief, not resolution. Wall Street had its best day since April: the S&P 500 rose 2.35% to 7,549.94 and the Nasdaq 100 3.40%, while Nvidia fell 4.27% as Trump said the FBI is ‘looking into’ Jensen Huang (CappNotes; FT). Oil did the heavy lifting: Brent settled 5.85% lower at $92.57, a ten-week low, as Trump called new Iran strikes ‘highly unlikely’, even as Saudi Arabia’s Hormuz-bypass pipeline was attacked (CappNotes; Al Jazeera). The VIX fell to 19.92. In South Africa the ANC fell to 30.7% and coalition talks have 13 days to run, yet the JSE rose 2.04% on gold and platinum miners and the ten-year eased to 8.41% (Sharenet; TE). Asia is extending the bounce: the Nikkei is +2.86% at 60,454 (FT, 05:43 GMT) and KOSPI +3.70% (TE). Today’s test is US October CPI at 13:30 GMT (consensus 3.9% y/y, TE), the last before a December Fed meeting where a hike is expected (Bloomberg); CoreWeave reports tonight. Two new chains: one on the pipe that carries the peace trade, one that reads the ANC’s rout as a bond story.
