Section 1What matters today

Two prices went in opposite directions on Monday, and that split is today’s story. The first was the AI trade. Meta’s new Muse AI agent reached the top of Apple’s free-app chart and set off a rush into chipmakers: AMD +10% to more than $1 trillion in market value, Intel +12%, Arm +17%, the Philadelphia chip index +4.3% and Meta itself +11% (Bloomberg, 21/9). The S&P 500 rose +1.49% to 7,764.70 and the Nasdaq 100 +2.83% to a record 30,482 (FRED, 21/9). Bitcoin reached an eight-month high above $86,000 (WSJ, 21/9). The second was fuel. Brent fell for a fourth day, to about $100, after Trump said he might meet Iran’s president at the UN (Bloomberg, 21/9). US diesel still hit a record $6.51 a gallon, and farm-state Republicans led by Senator Grassley are now asking the White House to ban diesel exports (FT, 22/9; Washington Examiner, 21/9). Cheaper crude has not made fuel cheaper, because the shortage is in refining. For your markets, the key day is tomorrow. The SARB decides with a hike now the consensus (Morgan Stanley, and Bank of America calling for two hikes this year), and August CPI is expected at 4.5% (Invezz, 21/9). Xi arrives in Washington the same day. Johannesburg is then shut on Thursday for Heritage Day, the day Trump and Xi meet.
