Section 1What matters today

The rout paused on Friday; the politics did not. Strong US jobs data (payrolls +165,000 against about 70,000 expected; unemployment down to 4.1%) gave tech a bounce: the Nasdaq 100 rose 1.35% to 26,989.99, but it still lost 4.91% on the week (MarketScreener; FT). Asia paid Thursday’s bill a day late: the Nikkei fell 5.96%, KOSPI 6.02%, and Taiwan entered a bear market (Bloomberg). India was the exception, with its best week since 2021. Over the weekend Nvidia’s Jensen Huang admitted an affair after the FT reported US officials believe the woman was a Chinese spy; Nvidia’s board has opened an investigation (FT). Oil fell 15% on the week to about $98 on Iran peace hopes (Authers, Bloomberg). In South Africa the DA was the most-voted party in five of eight metros (Moneyweb), and coalition deals must follow within 14 days. Asia is bouncing this morning: the Nikkei is +2.5% (FT, 05:30 GMT). Friday’s data settles five chains. SA bonds and GPU rents confirmed, and the bitcoin-ETF chain is dead. Two new chains follow the ballot box: one into GPU scarcity, one into SA’s domestic stocks. US CPI is out tomorrow at 13:30 GMT.
