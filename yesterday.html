Section 1What matters today

Asia took the hit that Wall Street dodged. Seoul’s KOSPI closed down 7.57% at 5,554.22 on Thursday after a circuit breaker, and the Nikkei fell 4.90% to 64,425.95 (Trading Economics; TradingKey), as the AI sell-off met a hot US inflation print. By New York’s close the mood had steadied: the S&P 500 rose 0.33% to 7,744.06 and the Nasdaq 100 slipped only 0.05% (AP; FT), while the ten-year Treasury yield fell 10bp to 5.09% as money ran to bonds (AP). This morning Tokyo is up 1.76% and Seoul about 3.4% (FT; Trading Economics). The new shock is Jakarta. MSCI says Indonesia’s place in its emerging-markets index is “no longer appropriate” and has opened a consultation on demoting it to a standalone market; Indonesian stocks fell about 11%, the steepest drop since January 1998, and the rupiah hit 18,070 per dollar, close to its record low (Reuters via MarketScreener). In London the FTSE 100 rose 1.08% to 10,840.35, its highest close since 26 August (Share Talk), but chancellor John Healey has now hinted at higher bank taxes in the 28 October Budget (FT, 16/10). The JSE gained 1.53% on miners and the rand firmed to 16.49. The thread running through it all is AI credit: CoreWeave’s 2032 bonds trade below 78 cents (Bloomberg) and data-centre bonds have fallen below par (FT). Today’s chains follow Indonesia’s index exile into the rupiah, test Seoul’s crash against Korea’s export data, and trace Healey’s bank surcharge to Investec on the JSE.

The FTSE 100 closed at its highest since 26 August as UK stocks outperformed the AI sell-off (Share Talk). Healey hinted at bank taxes in the Budget; one option restores the surcharge from 3% to 8% (FT). The FCA put Jarvis Investment Management and ePayments into special administration.

South Africa Miners lead, rand firmer

Resources led the JSE (Metals & Mining +2.60%) as palladium rose 3.35% and gold 1.17% (Sharenet). Moody’s says SA is on the path to an upgrade, and Bank of America says revenue could beat the budget by R70bn on the gold windfall (Investec; Business Day). Mondi fell 9.07% after its results.

United States Bonds bid, stocks steady

Stocks steadied after Wednesday’s rout as Treasuries rallied. Futures put the odds of an October Fed rise near even after CPI of 3.7% (Milk Road; Bloomberg). CoreWeave’s credit-risk gauge hit a record. Anthropic reported quarterly revenue of $16.8bn, up 50%, slower than some backers hoped (FT).
