Section 1What matters today

Inflation is back in charge. US October CPI beat forecasts for a third straight month: core prices rose 0.3% on the month and the headline 0.4% (Bloomberg). Fed governor Waller put a half-point hike on the table and Dallas Fed president Logan wants at least two more quarter-point rises; a December hike is now priced as a certainty, with an 11% chance of a jumbo (Authers, opinion). The two-year Treasury yield jumped 17bp to 4.73% and the ten-year 10bp to 4.98% (CappNotes). Stocks barely flinched: the S&P 500 fell 0.38% to 7,520.93 and the Nasdaq 100 0.60%, with Nvidia −5.47% (CappNotes). The bond sell-off travelled: the UK ten-year rose 8.9bp to 5.23% and the SA ten-year 9.5bp to 8.505% (TE), while gold fell 2.05% as the dollar index rose 0.58% (CappNotes). Into this, Anthropic set its IPO: up to $64bn at $70–78 a share (FT), or $90.4bn with a private placement, at about $1.4tn (Bloomberg); bankers may lift that to $1.5tn and more than $100bn (FT, this morning). In Korea, chip exports for 1–10 November rose 331.2% (Korea Customs via Korea Times), which settles last week’s blue-wave chip chain on its tripwire. Two new chains: a cancelled turbine order read as a resold slot, and a Korean trade surplus read as a currency story.

Asia Chips carry Korea

KOSPI rose 4.39% to 5,239.13 and the Nikkei 2.87% to 60,459.58 on Tuesday (TE; Bloomberg; Xinhua); TSMC’s monthly sales growth accelerated to 60% (Bloomberg). This morning the Nikkei is +1.31% and the Hang Seng −0.83% (FT, 05:30 GMT).

United Kingdom Gilts follow Treasuries

The FTSE 100 fell 0.29% to 10,751.59 and the FTSE 250 rose 0.2% (Share Talk). The ten-year gilt rose 8.9bp to 5.23% (TE). Sterling fixed at 1.3423 before the CPI print and was 1.3348 this morning (ECB; FT). Barclays, HSBC and JPMorgan are using credit insurance to stretch their data-centre lending limits (FT). Chancellor Healey faces the Treasury committee today; Q3 GDP is out on Thursday.

South Africa Jobs worse, Vodacom better

Unemployment rose to 33.7%, its highest since 2022 (Investec The Pulse), and September manufacturing fell 3.5% (Business Day). The ALSI slipped 0.30% to 114,693.44 as the Resource 10 fell 2.60% (DRDGOLD −6.87%, Harmony −5.26%), while Vodacom rose 7.15% to a record on its interims (Sharenet). The ten-year rose 9.5bp to 8.505% (TE).

United States Hot print, calm stocks
