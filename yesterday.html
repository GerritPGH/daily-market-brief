Section 1What matters today

The dollar did what the Fed did not. Fed futures barely moved on the October minutes, yet the dollar rallied to an 18-month high and JPMorgan’s emerging-currency gauge closed at its lowest since last year’s tariff sell-off (Points of Return, opinion). Robin Brooks says the move has “totally decoupled” from rate differentials, a sign that “something else is going on”. The damage was widest in South Africa: the rand broke R17, ending at 17.2226 on the ECB fix (+1.71%), and the All Share fell 3.66%, with banks down 4.64% and resources 5.99% (Sharenet). October CPI rose to 5.2% from 4.5% (Investec), and the SARB decides today; most economists expect a hold (BMR via Moneyweb). Bank Indonesia surprised with a hike to 6% to defend the rupiah (Bloomberg). Metals sold off hard: Comex gold −3.50% to $3,728.80, silver −4.84% and copper −5.24% to its lowest since April after Stan Druckenmiller turned bearish (CappNotes; Bloomberg). The minutes show almost all Fed officials expect more hikes, and Governor Waller floated a half-point move. Anthropic priced 980mn shares at $83, above its $70–78 range, raising $81bn, or about $94bn with the private placement, at roughly $1.5tn; it trades today as ANT (FT; Bloomberg). Nvidia still fell 2.89% (CappNotes). UK inflation came in below forecast at 3.6%, and the FTSE 100 fell 1.30% on miners (Share Talk). Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen today.

Asia Rupiah defended, yuan allowed to rise

KOSPI rose 1.99% to 5,067.44 and the Nikkei 0.28% to 59,024 (TE), but the Hang Seng fell 0.91% after Tencent’s revenue missed. Bank Indonesia unexpectedly raised its rate to 6%; 22 of 38 economists had expected a hold (Bloomberg). The PBOC set the yuan fix at 6.7118, its strongest since July 2022, as Shenzhen hosts APEC (Bloomberg). The Nifty slipped 0.63% to 25,227.95 (TE). This morning the Hang Seng is down about 2% and KOSPI 0.2% (TE; FT).

United Kingdom Miners drag; CPI softer than feared

The FTSE 100 fell 1.30% to 10,551.17, its third straight loss, as Antofagasta (−7.9%), Anglo American (−7.2%) and Glencore (−6.1%) tracked copper. Hays jumped 39% on a profit upgrade (Share Talk). October CPI was 3.6% against 3.7% expected, and services inflation of 3.7% came in under the Bank’s 3.9% projection (Yahoo UK). The two-year gilt fell 7.6bp to 4.92% and the ten-year to 5.29% (TE). Bailey says more hikes are needed if the Iran conflict lasts (Bloomberg).

United States Hawkish minutes; Anthropic priced high

The S&P 500 fell 0.23% to 7,323.63 and the Dow 1.11%, while the Nasdaq 100 rose 0.42% (CappNotes). Nvidia fell 2.89% after its beat and CoreWeave 4.13%; AMD rose 5.31%.
